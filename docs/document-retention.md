# Fixed document retention

The [bounded purge storage dependency](demo-purge-storage.md) provides exact per-envelope inventory/deletion authority. It is not registered or scheduled and does not yet remove SQL records; durable purge coordination remains required.

`Demo__PurgeAfterDays` is an optional positive integer from 1 through 36500. It remains unset in examples. Configure actual deployment values privately; the selected demo policy is seven days from the first successful document upload.

For a stored demo envelope, the first successful upload freezes one immutable `DocumentRetentionSnapshot` in the upload transaction. It records the policy version, selected days, UTC start and exact deletion deadline, and the first original/normalized object identities and hashes. The first upload audit binds the complete snapshot. A failed upload starts no interval; a committed upload whose acknowledgement is lost retains its original snapshot and readable objects.

The start uses whole UTC seconds, rounded down by less than one second at initial capture so the fixed deadline can be represented exactly by Blob Storage's RFC1123 retention timestamps. The audit keeps its actual event time. Publication and retries must not round or extend a frozen deadline.

Replacements preserve the first snapshot even if configuration changes or becomes unset. Replacement at or after its deadline is rejected. Concurrent first uploads use the common envelope lock and reviewed row version, so only the winning upload can establish the snapshot. Runtime SQL permissions permit snapshot reads/inserts and deny updates/deletes; EF also rejects tracked mutations. A later privileged purge path must deliberately remove these records.

Ordinary envelopes and unconfigured demo envelopes are not enrolled. Existing uploaded demo documents without a snapshot require an explicit migration decision before replacement after the setting is enabled. The application does not infer an earlier upload time, silently backfill them or reset their age. Reading a document does not create a snapshot.

The sender draft preview page, normalized PDF endpoint and explicit preview confirmation verify the frozen upload policy after sender authorization. At the exact cutoff they return an unavailable response (HTTP 410); the page omits document details, preview and upload/confirmation controls. Missing enrolled policy or invalid provenance returns a private verification failure (HTTP 503), without falling back to an unbounded ordinary read. Wrong or unrelated sessions gain no retention information. GET performs no enrollment, deadline change or purge scheduling.

PDF reads remain bounded and hash-verified before returning a file result, with a cutoff recheck after storage/hash work. Confirmation checks again under the envelope lock and after its audit write before commit. Already-started downloads and party-held copies cannot be recalled. Keep hosted retention disabled until remaining status/retrieval coverage, purge and disclosure/configuration work are complete. Internal worker/history verification and existing private storage capabilities are unchanged.

Sender signer setup, document details/recipients, field preparation and review also verify policy after complete sender/draft authorization. Their reads share the parent lock, with a final time check after loading private collections. Mutations check before preparation and again after their audit write, rolling back the whole change if the cutoff is crossed. At the exact cutoff the browser pages and field-save JSON endpoint return a private generic 410, without title, contact, parties, fields or disclosure data. Missing or inconsistent enrolled evidence returns a private generic 503. Earlier cookie expiry still applies; GET neither enrolls old records nor schedules work. Existing client-held drafts or downloads cannot be recalled. [Signer access and own-checkpoint confirmation](signer-access.md#fixed-demo-retention-during-signing) use their separate verified signer guard.

For a newly reviewed Send, the server caps signing expiry at the verified upload deadline. An earlier sender-selected expiry remains earlier; an absent or later selection uses the retention cutoff. Preparation review displays both the effective UTC expiry and retention deadline before affirmative Send. Its existing authenticated, CSRF-protected row-version form also carries the non-secret retention digest; a missing, forged or stale review cannot send an enrolled document. GET changes no saved deadline or work state.

Send rechecks the immutable policy under the common envelope lock and atomically freezes effective expiry with the source, disclosure, invitation and once-only [expiry work](signing-expiry.md). A versioned sent-audit extension binds the original requested expiry, effective expiry, cutoff and upload-policy digest. Canonical status/replay verifies that extension; ordinary audit serialization and historical evidence remain compatible. Deadline crossings during storage/audit preparation roll back before commit, and an unknown commit acknowledgement can recover the one committed request without extending its deadline.

This foundation does **not** schedule deletion, expire every read capability, or change hosted storage. [Final publication](final-publication.md) binds and preserves the exact upload deadline, verifies matching final-version protection and stores that deadline in `Envelope.PurgeAfter`. New publication at or beyond it fails closed. Ordinary/legacy completed records retain their established intervals and verification recipes. Complete remaining read coverage, purge orchestration, disclosure text and approved hosted configuration/validation before enabling this setting in the public demo.

Deletion must account for immutable blob versions, soft delete, backups and service logs. A logical deadline must not be described as immediate irreversible erasure of every recovery copy. No Azure/AWS resource or policy is created or changed by this code.
